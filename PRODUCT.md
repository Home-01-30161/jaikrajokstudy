# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React (TypeScript), TailwindCSS

## Users

Young Thai users seeking a safe, private space to log their emotions and mental state.

## Product Purpose

A private, judgment-free digital diary with integrated mental health support (like the 1323 hotline) for users to track their emotional wellbeing over time. Success means users feel safe, supported, and consistent in their reflection.

## Positioning

A highly personalized, aesthetic, and safe emotional reflection tool that bridges the gap between personal journaling and professional mental health resources in Thailand.

## Operating Context

Users access the app in private, vulnerable moments to reflect on their feelings, usually alone.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Requires legal guardian consent flows for users under 18.
- Must maintain strict privacy and safety (no public social sharing features).

## Brand Commitments

- Thai language interface.
- Specific collage-style analog aesthetic featuring grid backgrounds, a hand holding a pen (`hand_pen`), origami stars, and glasses.
- Warm, non-clinical, empathetic tone.

## Evidence on Hand

- Frontend implementation with Onboarding, Guardian Consent, Login, and Diary Logging views.
- Specific graphic collage assets locally tested and visually verified.

## Product Principles

1. **Safety First**: Never expose vulnerable emotional data or compromise user privacy.
2. **Empathetic Friction**: Guide users gently, especially minors who require guardian consent, without feeling bureaucratic.
3. **Tactile & Analog Feel**: Use the collage aesthetic to make the digital space feel like a real, personal diary rather than a sterile app.
4. **Immediate Support**: Always make mental health resources (like the 1323 hotline) highly visible when negative emotions are logged.
